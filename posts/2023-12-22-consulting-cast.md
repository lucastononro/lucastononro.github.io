Consulting Cast is a podcast run out of UFRJ, and it has hosted a long line of people from consulting and the executive world who know considerably more than I do. I was the guest on this one. It is in Portuguese, so if you do not read Portuguese, this post is the whole episode as far as you are concerned.

The conversation was about what advanced analytics and AI actually do to strategy work — not as a technology trend to put on a slide, but as something that changes which problems are worth taking on and how you go about solving them. I talked about my time at large strategy consultancies working on technology strategy, data science and machine learning, and what that suggests about the shape of the job for whoever comes next.

[Consulting Cast — UFRJ (in Portuguese)](https://open.spotify.com/episode/6vX9wZ4sz7IRfiNohFFtol)

## Explaining it without the jargon

The claim I'd make for an hour like this is a small one. I said the conversation wasn't about AI as a technology trend for a slide, and the reason that is hard to hold to is that the slide vocabulary does a lot of load-bearing work in this field. Take it away, in front of students and consultants who will ask what you actually mean, and you find out quickly which parts of the job you understand and which parts you have only heard described. Building the thing does not test that. Being made to explain it does.
