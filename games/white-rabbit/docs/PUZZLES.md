# Puzzle map

The complete comparison, source page references, corrections and deliberate 3D translations are in [FIDELITY-AUDIT.md](FIDELITY-AUDIT.md). That file contains spoilers.

The four regions follow the supplied game's sequence: the hall's story and size changes; four appointments in the woods; the Queen's gardens, trial and croquet; then the kings' construction and two perspective readings.

Puzzle definitions, accepted answers and optional hints live in `src/puzzles.js`. Shared geometry and construction rules live in `src/mechanics.js`. Their animated 3D presentation is in `src/spatial-puzzles.js`; the original Blender models are generated by `scripts/build_world.py` and `scripts/build_rabbit.py`.

English wordplay translates the French messenger sound puzzle to won/one and the tea invitation to tea/T. The Queen's English decree preserves the absent letters R, K and Z. Card bookkeeping becomes movement or object use; the gardeners' colored card header becomes a rose-painting interaction. These adaptations are documented explicitly instead of presenting them as literal copies.
