## Playability changes

The hall leads into a connected wooded valley, with bridges to appointment clearings, three routes through the Queen’s grounds, and a road to the house. Small discoveries require approaching their hiding places. Optional assisted walking reduces repeated travel without replacing free exploration. There is no countdown or wrong-answer penalty; hints escalate only when requested.

The rabbit has authored Idle, Hop and CheckWatch animation clips, attached ears and a watch attached to its paw. Its route waits for nearby players and follows current progress. The cook stirs, the Hatter and Queen have gentle head and arm movement, and the Cheshire eyes blink. These are stylized characters, not realistic scanned humans or animals.

The audit improves clue fairness and interaction variety. Automated tests and checkpoint play checks do not establish how entertaining or difficult the adventure will be for a first-time human player. A blind playtest remains the right next measure of pacing and difficulty.
