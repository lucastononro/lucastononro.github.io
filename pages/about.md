A notebook, not a publication. Posts are short on purpose. The bar is: *would
this have saved me a day two months ago?*

coding agents. **agents** is a bigger project. **talks** is me on someone else's
microphone. **research** is empty, which is honest.

## The site

Static HTML generated by a few hundred lines of Node in
[this repository](https://github.com/lucastononro/lucastononro.github.io) — no
framework, no build step beyond `node build.mjs`. Markdown in, HTML out.

Syntax highlighting and mathematics both render at build time, so no highlighter
and no maths library ship to your browser. Margin notes are CSS. Videos are
click-to-load — nothing is requested from YouTube until you press play, which is
how the no-cookies claim below stays true.

Set in [Alegreya](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alegreya) and Alegreya Sans,
with IBM Plex Mono for code.

**Tonon** means the lights are on. **Tonoff** means they are off, at which point
the site changes its name and I stop looking awake. Naming that switch took
longer than building it.

There is [an RSS feed](/feed.xml). There is no newsletter, no analytics, and no
cookie banner, because there are no cookies.
